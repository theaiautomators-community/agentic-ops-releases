# Agentic Ops 0.4.1

Released 24 November 2026.

A patch release on `v0.4.0`: the voice assistant builds apps and 3D scenes live on your screen while it talks you through them, a voice that knows when you have finished speaking, the Software Factory on your other projects, phone alerts straight from the app, an end to the app's long freezes on Windows, a quicker start, a stronger check for keys in your files, and a set of small fixes.

Members take it by asking their coding agent to update Agentic Ops. The full notes are in the community repository.
