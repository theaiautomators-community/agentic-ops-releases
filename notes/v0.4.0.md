# Agentic Ops 0.4.0

Released 20 November 2026.

A minor release on `v0.3.2`: a rebuilt voice assistant that works your screen while you watch, one standard for records and collections, a Software Factory board that checks and lands its own work, help pages for members, and many smaller improvements.

Members take it by asking their coding agent to update Agentic Ops. The full notes are in the community repository.
